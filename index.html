a little about me

A little about the person behind the tabs.

I’m a Business Administration student at the University of the People, building my path in customer support and virtual assistance.

Volunteering in my church’s sales unit taught me how much a good experience depends on the little things: clear answers, accurate records, a quick fix when something goes wrong, and a friendly follow-up. I helped customers find what they needed, kept the sales records (first in a notebook, later on the computer) and helped the bookshop manager reconcile sales and cash.

Since then I’ve been building hands-on skills in the everyday work of remote support: email, scheduling, task tracking and customer records. Now I’m looking for opportunities to put them to work.

01 Customer Support · Email

Delayed Order Response

Scenario

A customer contacts support because their order has not arrived within the expected delivery period. They are frustrated and want an update.

Skills demonstrated

Professional written communication Empathy and customer service Clear next steps Avoiding unconfirmed promises
Sample response
Subject Update on Your Delayed Order

Hello Michael,

Thank you for reaching out. I’m sorry your order hasn’t arrived within the expected delivery period. I understand how frustrating this must be.

I’ll check your order status and tracking information and get back to you with an update once I have more information.

Thank you for bringing this to our attention.

Kind regards,
Customer Support Assistant

02 Customer Support · Refunds

Damaged Item & Refund Request

Scenario

A customer received a damaged item and wants to know whether they can receive a refund. The refund policy has not yet been reviewed.

Skills demonstrated

Empathy and professional communication Following company policies Managing customer expectations Avoiding unconfirmed promises
Sample response
Subject Your Refund Request

Hello Sarah,

I’m sorry your item arrived damaged. I understand how disappointing this must be.

I’ll review our refund policy and check the next steps for your request. I’ll get back to you with an update once I’ve completed the review.

Thank you for bringing this to our attention.

Kind regards,
Customer Support Assistant

03 Customer Support · Order Issues

Wrong Item or Colour Received

A customer ordered a black bag but received a brown bag instead, and requests a replacement or refund.

I’m sorry you received the wrong colour of bag. I understand how disappointing this must be.

I’ll review your order details and check our replacement and refund policies to determine the available options. I’ll get back to you with an update once I’ve confirmed the next steps.

Kind regards,
Customer Support Assistant

04 Administrative Support

Administrative Support & Task Management

Scenario

A client asks me to confirm a supplier meeting, organise pending tasks, and keep track of follow-up actions.

Skills demonstrated

Calendar management and scheduling Task organisation and deadline tracking Digital document management Attention to detail Professional client communication
Example workflow
  1. 01 Check the client’s calendar and confirm their availability.
  2. 02 Contact or check with the supplier to verify availability before finalising the meeting.
  3. 03 Add the confirmed meeting date, time, and time zone to the calendar.
  4. 04 Organise outstanding tasks in Trello using To Do, Doing, and Done lists.
  5. 05 Add due dates and checklists to help track progress.
  6. 06 Update the task status when work is completed and communicate any important changes to the client.

Tools used

Google Calendar Scheduling meetings and setting reminders
Google Docs Preparing and organising documents
Google Sheets Tracking customer orders and follow-up tasks
Trello Organising tasks, deadlines, and checklists
